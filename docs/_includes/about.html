About Me

I'm a senior web developer with over 20 years of experience coding accessible, responsive, and maintainable web applications. My main focus is with front-end development, specializing in React and TypeScript supported by comprehensive unit testing practices.

Throughout my career, I've delivered high-quality solutions across government, nonprofit, education, and private industry sectors, integrating with REST APIs and building a strong working knowledge of back-end technologies including Node.js, Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL and MongoDB.

I'm also a self-taught banjo player. Having picked up initially playing 3-finger picking, Bluegrass style I have since shifted to Old-time clawhammer style. It gives me great enjoyment to learn new tunes and seek to improve my playing. Although I don't currently play on a regular basis with other musicians I hope to someday.
